**Last updated: [DATE]**

PlanBOQ ([your contact email]) built the PlanBOQ Android app. This policy
explains what the app does and does not collect.

## What the app processes

- **Floor plan files (DXF, PDF) and photos of plans** that you import are
  processed **on your device**. They are stored only in the app's private
  storage on your phone and are never uploaded to us.
- **Optional processing server**: if you configure a server URL in Settings,
  a downscaled image of your plan is sent to **that server** (which you or
  your organisation control) for line detection. PlanBOQ's developer does not
  receive or store it. Leave the server field empty to keep everything on-device.
- **Projects** you create are stored locally on your device. Deleting the app
  removes them.

## What we collect

- Nothing. The app has no analytics, no advertising, no trackers, and does not
  require an account. Google Play handles subscription payments; we never see
  your payment details.

## Permissions

- **Camera** — to photograph floor plans. Only used when you tap capture.
- **Photos/media (read)** — only the files you explicitly pick via the
  system picker are read.

## Subscriptions

Purchases are processed by Google Play. Subscription status is read from
Google Play on your device to unlock Pro features.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

Material changes will be reflected in the app's listing notes.
